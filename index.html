❤️ Thank You

We are grateful to everyone who contributed their time, resources, and energy—whether through giving donations, shopping, collecting boxes, packing boxes, or spreading the word.

A special thank you to our partners Storytime Crafts, Hometown Arcade, and the YMCA of Greater Boston.

We are proud to continue this tradition with you.

🗣️ Reflections from Our Team

The Toy Drive is my favorite because of the joy we bring to others during the holidays… Shopping is one of the best parts because we come out of the store with many carts full of toys… After we dropped off the toys at the YMCA, the director sent us reaction videos and it really warmed my heart.

The Toy Drive is my favorite part of the year. It's amazing seeing how much people donate, with the number growing each year… Being able to pass on that joy and seeing the impact a donated toy has really makes me appreciate everything even more.

It was shocking to see how many toys we collected this year… I love seeing the before and after and how much progress we've made each year.
