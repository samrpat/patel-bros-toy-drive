Questions

Short answers to what people ask most.

What kind of toys should I give?

New, unwrapped toys for kids in kindergarten through 8th grade.

When is the deadline?

All donations must be received by December 8.

Where do the toys go?

To a holiday store we create so the YMCA’s Partner Schools can make the holidays better for hundreds of K–8 kids.

What happens to Venmo donations?

Every dollar goes toward toys. We do the shopping for you.

Does this website track me?

We count visits and which buttons are used, so we can tell whether the site helps the drive. We don’t use cookies, we don’t collect names, emails or payment details, and we never see who you are. Payments happen entirely inside Venmo.
